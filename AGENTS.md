## Design Context

TaskFlow is a collaborative Kanban board (product register). Design guidance is captured in:

- **PRODUCT.md** — strategy, users, brand personality, anti-references, design principles
- **DESIGN.md** — visual system: colors, typography, elevation, components, do's and don'ts
- **`.impeccable/design.json`** — machine-readable tokens and component snippets

Key constraints: single-family Inter, flat-by-default with gray-200 borders, Indigo accent (#6366f1) used sparingly (≤10%), no shadows at rest, no gradient text or glassmorphism, no Jira/Linear/Notion aesthetic.
